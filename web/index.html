Cascade the interaction a pairwise check cannot see

Educational prototype, not clinical decision support. Predictions come from a static model built on the FDA's published interaction classes. They describe drug classes, not any individual patient, and must not be used to make or change treatment.

Brand names, misspellings, doses and pharmacy printouts are fine. A small model trained for this reads the list on the Cascade server; the text is not sent to any outside service. Every reading is shown so you can check it. It can misread a name, most often a medicine it has never seen.

Only drugs in the FDA interaction table can be scored. Anything else is listed as not covered rather than guessed at.
